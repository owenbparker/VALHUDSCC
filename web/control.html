Player cams

Players run the SCC Player Cam app on their PC, pick VALORANT, enter this PC's address (tick "Let other PCs on the network read the HUD" in Outputs) and their Riot ID. When the observer is spectating them, their cam pops up above the bottom left of the HUD, framed in black, with their name and K / D / A by a strip in their side's colour. In a 2v2 (Series & maps > Players) every player's cam stays up under their tab instead, showing the team logo until their cam connects.

Players

Links each player in the game to their player in the team's roster on GFX Engine (Teams > Team members), by their in-game name. Linked players show on the engine's graphics with their player name, headshot and flag.

Live data

Something read wrong? Save a frame at that moment (with the Tab scoreboard open, too) and send the files from the folder that opens.
Sending live data to the HUD

Anything that reads the match can POST JSON to /api/ingest on this PC, e.g. {"source": "reader", "match": {"round": 5, "phase": "combat", "score": {"left": 3, "right": 1}}, "players": [{"team": "left", "slot": 1, "agent": "Jett", "health": 80, "credits": 2300}]}. Only the fields that changed need sending. The full list is in the README.
